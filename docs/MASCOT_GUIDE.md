# Website mascot guide

**Selected direction:** Option C — Expressive cartoon, selected by the user on 5 November 2026. The source character kit v01 was completed on 8 November 2026.

**Name:** Crestie remains a working name. The selected direction is approved; individual kit variants were production-checked, not separately owner-approved.

The included [greeting pose](../public/assets/mascot/ostrich-greeting.png) is a static, transparent full-body PNG from that kit. It is suitable as a welcoming character placement. It is not a continuous waving animation.

## Keep consistent

Curious, warm and lightly witty. Preserve expressive dark-brown eyes, cream forehead tufts and long neck, an ochre beak, black-and-white plumage, green neckerchief, round gold clasp and two toes per foot. Use wing gestures and retain the original silhouette.

Keep the image undistorted, preserve transparency and leave space for feet and wing tips. Give content and controls clear space around the character. The original farm logo remains a separate asset.

For decorative placement use empty alternative text; for meaningful placement describe the character's role concisely. Respect reduced-motion preferences if movement is added later.

## Additional artwork

Only the greeting pose is bundled for initial website work. If another pose is needed, bring in that specific asset from the retained character kit, update docs/asset-manifest.json and check it in the final layout. For newly generated art, first obtain the approved three-quarter master and follow the full character rules; do not treat this single pose as a complete rig or turnaround sheet.
