The owner describes quality products and healthy, well-fed livestock. These are owner-supplied brand statements, not veterinary assessments or certifications. A nationwide audience does not establish nationwide delivery.

## Visual identity and tone

- Forest green: #0C230F; cream: #FEF8EC; gold: #C3922C; gold highlight: #EDC56E.
- Existing content uses Georgia headlines and Arial body text. These are a useful starting point for the website.
- Warm, clear and grounded, with accessible agricultural education for a general Nigerian audience.
- Preserve the supplied logo. Use the expressive ostrich mascot as a supporting personality; its working name, Crestie, is unapproved.
- Use the supplied artwork without stretching or presenting illustrations as real farm photography.

## Details still to establish during the website build

Current products and availability; final phone/WhatsApp or email; delivery/collection arrangements; owner-approved farm photographs; hosting and domain access.

Do not invent missing details, prices, opening hours, certifications, testimonials, awards or fulfilment promises. Domain and social identities above were supplied by the owner; this repository setup did not verify live accounts, domain control or website availability.

## Website decisions confirmed 10 November 2026

- Primary purpose: explore the farm and enquire about products. The site is not an online checkout.
- The owner said the WhatsApp number is not ready and expressly authorised placeholders for this local build.
- The initial preview used “WhatsApp — Coming soon”. The owner subsequently marked that notice for removal; WhatsApp now stays hidden. `public/site-config.js` holds a null value until the owner supplies a real number.
- The supplied Instagram profile is the available link in the local preview. A visitor can prepare and copy an enquiry, then open the profile. Instagram response times, account availability and ordering policy have not been confirmed. No message is sent by the website.
- Local development and preview only. GitHub publishing and deployment remain separate.
